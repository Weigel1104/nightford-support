Support

Nightford (渡夜) is an original pixel-art wuxia action roguelike. Pick a hero, fight through procedurally generated stages with flowing combos, collect Soul Seals to build your style, defeat each stage’s guardian boss, then descend into ever-harder Long Night difficulties.

  • DevicesiPhone with iOS 17.0 or later
  • How it playsLandscape, fully offline
  • PricingOne-time purchase. No ads, no in-app purchases
  • AccountNone needed — no sign-up, no login

FAQ

Where is my progress saved?

All progress and settings are stored only on your iPhone, in the game’s built-in local storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server. This includes your run stats, Long Night progress, achievements, Soul Jade and Scripture Pavilion unlocks, codex, and sound and brightness settings.

If I delete the game, can I get my progress back?

No. Deleting the app also deletes all game progress on that device. We have no servers and keep no copies, so we can’t restore it for you.

Can I move my progress to a new iPhone?

There is no cloud save or account sync at the moment. If you move to a new iPhone using an iCloud or computer backup, your progress may come along with the backup, but that depends on how iOS handles backups and we can’t guarantee it. Also, when a device is critically low on storage, iOS may clear an app’s local data, so it’s a good idea to keep some free space.

How do I reset my progress and start over?

There is no “erase all progress” button in the game yet; it will be added together with the upcoming settings screen. For now, to start fresh, delete and reinstall the game (this erases all progress permanently).

If you only want to respec, Inner Arts in the Scripture Pavilion can be reset for free at any time, and all Soul Jade you spent is refunded.

Do I need an account or to sign in?

No. There is no account system — just open the game and play.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game runs completely offline, even in Airplane Mode.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Nightford is a one-time paid game with no ads and no in-app purchases. Soul Jade is earned only by playing and can’t be bought with real money.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Use the Sound button on the main menu or the pause menu. Vibration during combat comes from your iPhone’s haptic feedback; to turn it off, disable System Haptics under Settings › Sounds & Haptics on your iPhone.

The game froze or crashed. What should I do?

Fully close the game from the app switcher and reopen it, and make sure your iPhone is on iOS 17.0 or later. If the problem continues, email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, game version (shown under About on the main or pause menu) and what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
