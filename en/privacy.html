Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS game Nightford (夜劍; the “Game”) and this support website treat your information. The Game is developed and published by independent developer Wang Wai Ng (“we”, “us”).

Summary

  • The Game does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information, and it does not track you.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. We cannot read them.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Deleting the app deletes all game data.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game does not collect your personal information, store it on any server, or transmit it to anyone. This includes, without limitation: your name, email address, phone number, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, IDFA), IP address, gameplay or usage data, and diagnostic or crash data.

The Game requires no sign-up or login, and it does not request any system permission such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, notifications or tracking.

2. Data stored on your device

To remember your progress and settings, the Game saves the following on your device using the local storage (localStorage) of the app’s built-in web view. This data is used only by the Game itself; it never leaves your device and we cannot access it.

Future versions may add new on-device save data (for example a mid-run save or more settings). It will likewise be stored only on your device.

3. Network connections

The Game runs entirely offline and does not connect to any server; all game content is bundled inside the app. The only time this website opens is when you tap “Privacy Policy” or “Support” on the in-game About screen, which opens your browser. Tapping the contact email opens your mail app.

4. Third-party services and SDKs

The Game contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social-login or tracking SDKs.

The Game uses the open-source Capacitor framework to package the game as an iOS app, and its haptics plug-in to produce light vibrations during combat. Both run only on your device and neither collects nor transmits data. The Game does not engage in tracking as defined by Apple’s App Tracking Transparency.

5. Information handled by Apple

When you download and purchase the Game from the App Store, purchase and payment information is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We never see your identity or payment details.

If you have turned on Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Share With App Developers on your iPhone, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics with us that do not identify you personally. This sharing is controlled by Apple and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

6. When you contact us

If you email us at polaris.research2000@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include (for example your device model or screenshots). We use this only to reply to you and resolve your issue. We do not use it for marketing, and we do not sell or share it with third parties unless required by law.

Correspondence is kept in our mailbox no longer than necessary once your issue is resolved, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. The support mailbox is provided by Gmail, so emails are stored on Google’s servers (which may be located outside your country or region, for example in the United States) and are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. As the hosting provider, GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes; see About GitHub Pages and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Children’s privacy

The Game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device: delete the app to permanently delete it. If you use iCloud Backup or computer backups, those backups may contain this data; they are managed by iOS and you can manage or delete them in Settings.
  • Email correspondence: email us and ask us to delete it.

Apart from this we hold no data about you, so there is no server-side data to access, export or delete.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California consumer privacy law (CCPA/CPRA)), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information and to object to its processing. Because the Game collects no personal information, we generally hold no data about you; for information you send us by email, contact us at any time to exercise these rights. Our legal basis for handling your emails is responding to your request (legitimate interests). If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

We do not sell personal information and do not “share” it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

11. Changes to this policy

If we add features that process data in the future (for example Game Center leaderboards and achievements, or iCloud saves), we will update this policy and the privacy information on the App Store before that version is released, and update the effective date on this page.
